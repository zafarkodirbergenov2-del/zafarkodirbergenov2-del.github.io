Jarvis

A private personal assistant application, used by a single person — its own developer and owner.

What it does

Jarvis runs on the owner's own computer and is operated through a private Telegram bot. It helps with everyday work: keeping track of tasks, preparing documents, and answering questions.

Google services it uses

  • Google Calendar — to show the owner their schedule and to add or move their own events when asked.
  • Gmail — to show the owner their recent mail, and to draft or send replies on their explicit instruction.

Jarvis only ever connects to the account of the person running it. It is not offered as a service to anyone else, and it has no other users.
